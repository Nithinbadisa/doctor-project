Common questions

View all FAQs →
Does MindCare store my assessment answers?

No. Assessment questions, answers, scores, and results stay in HealthPlix and are not sent back to this public site.

Can I explore services before creating an account?

Yes. Browse service and therapy information here without creating a MindCare account.

Are online self-checks a diagnosis?

No. A self-check is preliminary information and does not replace evaluation by a qualified clinician.
