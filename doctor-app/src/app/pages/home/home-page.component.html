Common questions

Know what happens before you continue.

View all FAQs →
Does Antara store my check-in answers?

No. Your selections and total stay only in the current page session and are not saved or sent. Formal clinical assessments are handled in HealthPlix.

Can I explore services before creating an account?

Yes. Browse service and therapy information here without creating an Antara account.

Are online self-checks a diagnosis?

No. Antara check-ins are not validated screening tools and do not replace evaluation by a qualified clinician.
